Understanding the Nine Human Design Centers

May 30, 2025✦2 min read✦Gabriela Trifonova

In Human Design, the nine energy centers are the core of your personal blueprint. They represent different aspects of human experience, from thinking and communication to identity, emotions, and physical vitality. Whether defined (colored in on your chart) or undefined (white), each center plays a vital role in how you process energy, interact with others, and make decisions.

Understanding how these centers function can help you navigate your life with more awareness, compassion, and authenticity.

Head Center

  • Theme: Inspiration, questions, and mental pressure

  • Defined: Consistent way of processing inspiration and mental pressure

  • Undefined: Open to various ideas and questions from the outside world

Ajna Center

  • Theme: Conceptualization, beliefs, and mental certainty

  • Defined: Fixed way of thinking and processing information

  • Undefined: Flexible thinking and open-mindedness, but can be susceptible to mental pressure

Throat Center

  • Theme: Expression, communication, manifestation

  • Defined: Consistent style of communicating and expressing ideas

  • Undefined: Adaptive communication style; seeks attention to be heard

Learn About Your Human Design Profile 

G Center (Identity Center)

  • Theme: Love, identity, direction in life

  • Defined: Stable sense of self and direction

  • Undefined: Fluid identity; takes in love and direction from others

Discover Your Strategy in Human Design

Heart Center (Will/Ego Center)

  • Theme: Willpower, self-worth, material drive

  • Defined: Strong will and drive; consistent access to willpower

  • Undefined: Learning to manage commitments and self-worth

Solar Plexus Center

  • Theme: Emotional awareness, emotional waves, feelings

  • Defined: Emotional authority; clarity comes over time

  • Undefined: Sensitive to others' emotions; experiences emotional openness

Read: Human Design Authority – Your Decision-Making GPS

Sacral Center

  • Theme: Life force, work, sexuality, generative power

  • Defined: Sustainable energy for work and life (only for Generators and MGs)

  • Undefined: Must manage energy carefully; not a consistent power source

Spleen Center

  • Theme: Intuition, instinct, immune system, fear

  • Defined: Reliable intuitive knowing and survival awareness

  • Undefined: Highly intuitive in the moment, but inconsistent

Root Center

  • Theme: Pressure to act, adrenaline, momentum

  • Defined: Consistent handling of stress and pressure

  • Undefined: Feels external pressure to move or finish things quickly

Defined vs Undefined Centers

All of us have all nine centers—whether they are defined or undefined. Defined centers (colored in) indicate areas where we consistently generate or express energy. Undefined centers (white) are areas where we absorb, amplify, and learn from the energy around us.

Understanding these differences helps you recognize what's authentically yours versus what's being taken in from your environment. The dance between the defined and undefined centers makes each chart—and each person—entirely unique.
